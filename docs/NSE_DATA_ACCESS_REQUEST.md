# NSE Original Research Data Access Request

NSE's official Data for Research program describes approved academic
and noncommercial access via its **Data Seeking Request Form**, which
you must truthfully complete and send to **nseri@nse.co.in**.

Official pages:
https://www.nseindia.com/static/research/initiatives-data-for-research
https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/nse-data-policy

The policy may require additional permission for construction or
distribution of derived financial indexes. File access alone is not
permission to publish raw licensed market data.

## Request email template (YOU must send)

Subject: NIFTY 50 quantitative index research — historical data and use permission

Dear NSE Economic Policy Research Team,

I am conducting a reproducible noncommercial quantitative study of a
multi-factor NIFTY 50 research index. I will compare transparent factor
index rules against the dividend-inclusive NIFTY 50 benchmark, estimate
benchmark-relative risk and execution-capacity assumptions, and investigate
bias caused by survivorship, source revision and corporate actions.

For [START DATE] through [END DATE], please advise the permitted process
for accessing: original index additions/deletions and effective dates,
historical securities and benchmark constituent weights; NIFTY 50 TRI
levels; dated prices/dividends/splits/mergers/delisting proceeds; first-
published filing vintages and historic market caps; sector classification,
factor inputs and dated volume/market-impact research data.

I would like guidance on the required Data Seeking Request Form,
eligibility, pricing, confidentiality and licensing. Please clarify
whether the approved use covers a research-only derived comparison index,
privately stored summaries, sharing reproducible Python code without
raw NSE data, academic charts and a noncommercial résumé demo.

My truthful current affiliation and purpose are [AFFILIATION AND PURPOSE].
I will respect any data access restrictions and publication terms.

Thank you,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR CONTACT DETAILS]

## Owner responsibilities

Use the official form and verified identity; only you can accept the
data-license terms or authorize payment. Do not attach broker tokens,
client holdings or third-party secrets. Retain the approval and
source-version receipt privately for independent review.
